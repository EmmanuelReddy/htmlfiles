1. Role Surfaces, Dashboards, and Interlinks

The portfolio system starts from role dashboards. GP and analyst operate funds and companies, LP and angel get investment-facing views, and founder works through an own-company workspace. Inbox and meetings sit beside these role surfaces.

5Tenant roles: GP, analyst, LP, angel investor, founder.
3Main write moments: fund creation, company addition, investment or round close.
4Fan-out channels: email, inbox, dashboards, published LP reports.

GP

Fund operator

Creates and manages funds, companies, reports, LP communication, signals, and raise-round investment views.

Analyst

Operations and review

Works through portfolio tabs and analyst queues for approvals, reports, documents, MCA, LP capital, and company review.

LP

Fund-position reader

Sees commitments, cashflows, holdings, capital calls, and published reports for funds where membership exists.

Angel

Co-investment reader

Sees co-investment performance, company detail, SPV detail, lead fund context, and raise-round opportunities.

Founder

Own-company workspace

Sees only companies connected through founder ownership and updates reports, funding, investors, documents, and raise status.

2. Fund Created Flow

A new fund starts from GP/analyst portfolio write access. The system creates the fund, invalidates fund cache, provisions LP invite/membership records, creates LP inbox actions, sends LP email, and notifies analysts so the operational team sees the new fund immediately.

Creates the fund

Fund rows are created through portfolio write routes and become the fund context for companies, LP commitments, reports, and metrics.

LP

Receives invite and inbox action

Existing LPs can be activated directly. New LPs receive a join token and an inbox item that accepts the fund invite.

Analyst

Gets notified

Active analysts get an inbox acknowledgement plus a fund update email so operations can track the newly created fund.

4. Role Reaction Map

Use this table to explain what each role sees when the fund or company lifecycle changes. GP and analyst operate the portfolio. LP and angel receive scoped investment views. Founder receives only own-company workspaces after acceptance.

Scenario GP Analyst LP Angel Founder Main shared systems
Fund created Creates active fund and selects fund context in portfolio. Receives inbox/email and can operate the fund in analyst surfaces. Receives invite email and inbox accept action; sees fund after membership. No direct effect until invited as co-investor or member. No direct effect until a company ownership link exists. Fund record, fund membership, invite links, inbox items, email dispatcher, cache invalidation.
Company added Sees company in portfolio list/detail and can manage company context. Receives notifications and can review queues for docs, MCA, reports, approvals. Top LP gets email/inbox; LP holdings and reports reflect the company when published. Gets invite/email/inbox if added as co-investor; views update after membership. Gets founder invite/email/inbox; workspace appears after acceptance. Company record, portfolio company record, founder invitations, role invites, inbox, email, optional investment and valuation.
Investment or round close KPIs, MOIC, reports, company detail, and portfolio AI update. Approval queues clear or change; valuation/report/capital views refresh. NAV, TVPI, DPI, IRR, cashflows, holdings, and reports update by fund. Co-investment cards, SPV/detail pages, and activity feeds update. Funding history, investors, cap table, and raise status update. Investments, valuation marks, ownership snapshots, fund metric recompute, LP report refresh, dashboards.
MIS, documents, signals Sees anomalies, documents, reports, alerts, and operational KPIs. Reviews submissions, document requests, anomalies, and approval outcomes. Sees only approved/reportable outputs through LP reporting. Sees only scoped co-investment activity and allowed document attention. Submits MIS/documents and sees own-company action requests. Change requests, MIS submissions, documents, anomaly alerts, dashboard feeds, email notifications.
