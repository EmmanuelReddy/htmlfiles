§ 02 — Role Profiles
What Each Role Can Do

LP, Analyst, Founder, and Angel are all new roles — none existed in the initial scope.

Fund manager · 44 permissions · GPDashboard

View portfolio only→Full fund lifecycle management
No invite flows→LP / Angel / Founder orchestration
No financial depth→TVPI · DPI · RVPI · IRR · MOIC · burn · runway

Fund Operations

  • Fund Creation — hard-cap, close dates, LP invite batch
  • LP Management — add/revoke, committed INR, equalization interest
  • Company Addition — triggers inbox across all 5 roles
  • Capital Activity — deployments, distributions, call scheduling

Portfolio Intelligence

  • LP Reports — AI-generated quarterly reports, publish & email
  • Signals & Alerts — anomaly detection, proactive insights, saved queries
  • Raise Rounds — investor discovery, angel invites, round publication

§ 03 — Business Flows
Key End-to-End Flows

Major cross-role flows that did not exist in the initial scope.

🏦 Fund Creation → LP Invites

  • 1GP creates fund with hard-cap, GP commitment, first/final close dates.
  • 2System validates: total LP committed + GP ≤ hard-cap.
  • 3Fund created; inbox notifications sent to all GPs & analysts.
  • 4Per LP invite: existing member → FundMembership; new user → email invite.
  • 5LP accepts → equalization interest computed, close type recorded.
  • 6Final-close gate: new member adds blocked after final_close_at.

🏢 Company Addition → Multi-Role Notifications

  • 1GP or Analyst adds a company to a fund.
  • 2Company and PortfolioCompany records created.
  • 3Inbox items created for: Founder, co-investing Angels, top LP, all Analysts, all GPs.
  • 4Founder receives email invite if not already a platform member.
  • 5Each role sees a role-appropriate action in their Inbox.

📋 MIS Submission — Founder → Analyst

  • 1Founder submits MIS data for their company.
  • 2Change Request created (pending); analyst alerted immediately.
  • 3Analyst reviews in the MIS Review Queue.
  • 4Approve → MIS record created, MOIC/burn recomputed, anomaly check runs.
  • 5Reject → Founder notified, can resubmit with corrections.
  • 6Anomaly flags surface on Founder's own company dashboard.

📣 Raise Round — Founder → Angel → GP

  • 1Founder creates a raise round (draft) with target amount and close date.
  • 2Founder publishes → core fields locked via change-requests engine.
  • 3Angels browse public rounds or receive private invites.
  • 4Angel responds: interested / meeting_requested → founder notified.
  • 5GP/Analyst reviews the responded-invites list.
  • 6Analyst closes round → Investment + CapitalActivity created, fund metrics recomputed.

📹 Meetings → Transcript → AI Summary

  • 1Any role creates a meeting; invitees auto-notified by email.
  • 2LiveKit room opens; participants join via JWT room token.
  • 3Recording saved to S3 automatically.
  • 4Transcript generated asynchronously in the background.
  • 5LLM produces a concise meeting summary.
  • 61h reminder email sent; Google Calendar sync runs best-effort.

📊 LP Portal — Fund Position View

  • 1LP logs in and lands on the LPPortal dashboard.
  • 2Views all member funds: NAV, MOIC, IRR at a glance.
  • 3Drills into a fund: cashflows, holdings, LP analytics.
  • 4Receives capital-call notification → confirms wire transfer.
  • 5Downloads the published LP quarterly report as PDF.
  • 6Cross-org statement aggregates positions across all funds & orgs.

Left to Implement — Fund Creation

Mid-fund LP joins

When a new LP joins a fund that is already active, the system needs to recalculate metrics, equalization interest, and ownership splits across all existing members.

Shareholding redistribution

When new capital is added to an existing fund, each LP's percentage holding needs to be recalculated and reflected across reports and dashboards.

Final round close mechanics

When the fund hits its hard cap and closes, all metrics — TVPI, DPI, RVPI, IRR — need to be finalised and locked. Currently these refresh on a monthly schedule, not instantly on close.

LP exit process

If an LP exits the fund, how their committed capital and returns are settled, how their stake is distributed to remaining members, and how the cap table and reports are updated — this full flow is not yet built.

Company Added Flow

A company is first added as a portfolio company in pending founder activation. The system then fans out to founder onboarding, angel co-investor invitations, top-LP notification, analyst notifications, and fund metric cache invalidation.

Left to Implement — Company Module

Company exit flow

When a company exits the portfolio (acquisition, IPO, write-off), how ownership percentages, MOIC, and final returns are calculated and recorded across all stakeholders — GP, LP, and angel — is not yet built.

Shareholding waterfall on exit

On an exit event, returns need to flow through a waterfall — preferred returns to LPs first, then GP carry, then remaining to angels and common holders. This distribution logic is not yet implemented.

Cap table on new funding rounds

When a company raises a new external round, the cap table needs to reflect dilution for existing holders and add new investor rows. This update flow needs to be connected end-to-end.

Metrics refresh on approval

After an MIS submission is approved, company-level metrics should update immediately. Currently they only refresh on the monthly schedule — the instant refresh path is not yet wired.

Add company process improvements

The company addition flow needs guardrails — duplicate company checks, sector and stage validation, and a clearer activation handoff between GP and founder before the company is marked live in the fund.
